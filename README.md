# DeFlock Indiana

DeFlock Indiana is an independent awareness project focused on the growing use of Automated License Plate Readers (ALPRs) throughout Indiana.

The goal of this website is to make information about ALPR surveillance easier for Indiana residents to understand and explore. The site highlights where cameras have been documented, explains how ALPR technology works, and provides information about the privacy and civil-liberty concerns surrounding large-scale license plate surveillance.

## What This Site Does

DeFlock Indiana provides:

- Information about Automated License Plate Readers (ALPRs)
- Documented ALPR and Flock Safety camera counts across Indiana
- City-level information about documented cameras
- Examples of reported ALPR misuse and privacy concerns
- Links to resources where people can explore, identify, and report cameras

## Shoutout to DeFlock

This project is heavily inspired by and built around the work of [DeFlock](https://deflock.org/).

DeFlock is an open-source project working to document Automated License Plate Readers and make their locations visible to the public. Their community-built tools and map make it possible for people to better understand the scale of ALPR surveillance in their own communities.

You can explore their work at:

- [DeFlock.org](https://deflock.org/)
- [DeFlock Camera Map](https://maps.deflock.org/)
- [Report an ALPR](https://deflock.org/report)
- [Find DeFlock Groups](https://deflock.org/groups)

## About This Project

DeFlock Indiana is an independent project focused specifically on Indiana. It is not the official DeFlock website and is not affiliated with Flock Safety.

Camera counts and locations may change over time and should be treated as community-documented information rather than a definitive inventory of every ALPR operating in Indiana.

## Why?

People should be able to understand the surveillance technology operating in their communities.

This project exists to make that information easier to find.
